Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

This policy explains how Spherical-Lab Innovation Private Limited ("we", "us") handles personal information when you use our WhatsApp guest concierge service (the "Service"), which we operate for participating hotels. The Service lets guests of those hotels message on WhatsApp for help during their stay, such as requesting housekeeping, amenities, or information about the hotel.

1. Information we collect

  • Contact details: your name and the WhatsApp phone number you use to message us.
  • Booking details: your room number and the dates of your stay.
  • Messages and media: the content of messages you send us, including any photos, voice notes, documents or locations you choose to share.
  • Delivery information: technical status such as whether a message was sent, delivered or read, and timestamps.

We do not ask for payment card details or government ID through this Service. Please do not send them.

2. How we use your information

  • To respond to your requests and deliver hotel services during your stay.
  • To send a welcome message and service messages related to your booking.
  • To route requests to the right hotel team and keep a record of what was requested.
  • To maintain the security, quality and reliability of the Service.

3. Automated and AI-assisted replies

Replies in this Service may be generated by automated systems, including artificial intelligence. They are intended only for hotel-related requests. Hotel staff may review conversations and may step in to help you directly. Automated replies can be wrong, so please confirm anything important with the front desk. In an emergency, contact local emergency services or the front desk directly rather than using this Service.

4. Who we share information with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with:

  • Meta / WhatsApp, which carries the messages. Their handling of your data is governed by WhatsApp's own terms and privacy policy.
  • Service providers that help us run the Service, such as cloud hosting, database and AI model providers, who may process information only on our instructions.
  • The hotel where you are staying and its staff, who need the information to fulfil your requests.
  • Authorities, where required by law.

5. How long we keep information

We keep conversation and booking records for 90 days after your check-out date, unless we or the hotel are required by law to keep them longer. After that they are deleted or anonymised.

6. International transfers

Our providers may process information in countries other than your own. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for such transfers.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections and access controls. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your information, object to or restrict certain processing, and withdraw consent. You can stop receiving messages at any time by telling us in the chat or by emailing us. To exercise any right, see our data deletion page or contact us below.

9. Children

The Service is intended for adult guests. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 18.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date above shows when it last changed.
