Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: 26 November 2026

You can ask Spherical-Lab Innovation Private Limited to delete the personal information we hold about you from our WhatsApp guest concierge service, which we operate for participating hotels.

How to request deletion

  1. Email abu.samar@spheicallab.com with the subject "Delete my data".
  2. Include your name, the WhatsApp phone number you used, and your stay dates, so we can find your records.
  3. We may ask you to confirm your identity, for example by replying from the same WhatsApp number, to protect your privacy.

What happens next

  • We will confirm receipt of your request and complete it within 30 days.
  • We delete your contact details, booking details linked to the Service, and your chat messages and media from our systems.
  • We may keep limited information where the law requires it, for example for accounting, security or dispute purposes, and only for as long as necessary.
  • Copies held by WhatsApp / Meta on your own phone or in your account are controlled by you and by WhatsApp, not by us.

Stopping messages

If you only want to stop receiving messages, tell us in the chat or email us, and we will stop contacting you through the Service.

More about how we handle information is in our Privacy Policy.
